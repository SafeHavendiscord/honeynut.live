PHOTOS FOLDER
=============
Drop pictures in here with any names (.jpg .jpeg .png .webp .gif).
They show in the Photography window in file-name order.

Like the music folder, this needs a files.json listing the photos
(make it with List Maker while signed in as HoneyNUT, or tools/make-lists.sh).

Tip: shrink phone photos to about 1600 px wide so the page loads fast.
